Radiant Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts tubing with multiplication and division: the length a floor takes at a spacing, the whole loops and what the last one carries, the manifolds and the spare ports, and the total flow from the per-loop flow. The area, the spacing, the loop limit, the manifolds and the flow are yours to measure or read off your own plan and parts. It applies the counting as written, so it has not read your heat loss and does not know your floor build-up, your water temperature, your manifold location or your pump curve, and it gives no verdict that a layout heats the room. Every number here is your own entry.

The tubing truth

Tubing length: the area times twelve over the on-center spacing.

The loops truth

Whole loops: rounded up, and what the last one carries.

The manifold truth

Manifolds: the loops over the ports, rounded up, and the ports left spare.

The flow truth

Total flow: the loops times the per-loop flow.

Radiant Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
