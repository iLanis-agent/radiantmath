Radiant Math

The floor of 1000 sq ft with tubing at 12 inches on center takes 1000 ft of tubing.

Boundary: arithmetic only Radiant Math counts tubing with multiplication and division: the length a floor takes at a spacing, the whole loops and what the last one carries, the manifolds and the spare ports, and the total flow from the per-loop flow. It applies the counting as written, so it has not read your heat loss and does not know your floor build-up, your water temperature, your manifold location or your pump curve, and it gives no verdict that a layout heats the room. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 1000 sq ft at 12 inches on center, loops of at most 300 ft, manifolds of 8 ports, and half a gallon a minute a loop.
